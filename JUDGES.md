you the exact command.** Nothing in our materials is hand-typed; tables are

## 5-minute path

1. `python verify.py` — watch every receipt re-derive offline (~seconds).
2. Open the runnable demo (link in submission) — click relics; the HUD shows
   each asset's receipt badge flipping to `PASS ✓` as you verify them.
3. Click the second pack button — the **same level re-themes live** from
   `sci-fi-outpost.yaml`.
4. Read `bench/benchmark.md` — the measured boundary table, including our
   published failure taxonomy.
5. Skim `assetforge/gates.py` — the deterministic verdicts are ~150 lines of
   readable stdlib code, not a black box.

## Honest limits

- Populated from the actual sweep in `bench/benchmark.md` — 22/22 coarse production gates passed, including every vendor-documented weak category. The recorded miss in our corpus is the probe's lower-bound under-delivery (task 8397194, in `artifacts/ledger.jsonl`). Coarse gates deliberately do not judge fine-feature fidelity (small text, faces, carving quality) — those remain the vendor's documented weak zones, and our pack briefs route around them.
- Watertight gating depends on trimesh; if unavailable, the gate is SKIPPED
  and marked as such in receipts — never guessed.

## What this is for (the New Species case)

The organizer's own words: *an ecosystem formed around toolchains, scenarios,
and developers is the barrier harder to replicate.* AssetForge is that
toolchain layer for Lux3D, shipped: an installable CLI, a measurable benchmark,
an open receipts format, and a game proving the loop end-to-end.
